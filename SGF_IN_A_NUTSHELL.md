# SGF IN A NUTSHELL

## WHAT IS SGF

SGF is an architecture for grounded machine meaning: it turns prose and other inputs into structured, source‑traceable, claim‑bearing objects that machines can store, query, transport, and govern. It does not try to capture all human meaning or solve consciousness; it focuses on meaning that can be grounded to identifiers, structured into Synapses, traced to sources, carried across trust boundaries, filtered by Trust Lenses, and admitted or refused by a receiver.

You can read it as:

> The Semantic Web, for real this time: Synapses instead of triples, a fixed 15‑role grammar instead of predicate explosion, and a typed governance language that can actually stop unsafe acts.

### The semantic substrate

- Core Lexicon and Canonical IDs give shared sense‑level addresses for meaning, with polyhierarchic ISA chains down to a Prime Registry of about 65 semantic primitives.  
- Synapses are the atomic units of structured meaning: one VerbHub plus role‑bound Spokes from a fixed set of 15 semantic roles, keeping “who did what to whom, when, where, how, and why” together as one claim‑bearing object.  
- Frames and Groups layer epistemic, rhetorical, normative, and perspective context on top (fact vs allegation vs rule vs fiction, whose point of view, which TrustLens), and assemble larger arguments, narratives, and rule sets as graphs instead of flat documents.

### The protocol and governance layers

- HFF (Hub Fact Format) is the wire format that carries Synapses, Canonical IDs, micro‑lexicons, provenance, hashes, and signatures so meaning can move between systems as sealed claim‑containers rather than loose fields.  
- AFP (Act and Federation Protocol) declares what each message is doing as a communication move – INFORM, ADVISE, QUERY, REQUEST, COMMAND, PROPOSE, ACCEPT, REFUSE, PROMISE, CONFIRM, CANCEL, ACK, ERROR – so machines exchange not just content but participation moves, under receiver sovereignty.  
- Omega is a typed governance language that lets systems express must, must‑not, may, exceptions, and mutation as rules over SGF objects and AFP acts, and compile those rules into checks that run before a plan or COMMAND is admitted.

## WHAT SGF MAKES POSSIBLE

SGF is a substrate; it is meant to be shared by many independent systems, not owned by any one vendor. By giving machines grounded atoms of meaning, a protocol for admissible messages, and a typed governance layer, it makes several classes of systems more realistic or safer to build.

### Governed AI operating systems

- SGF OS (and any peer OS) can treat SGF as its meaning and evidence layer: Synapses, Frames, Canonical IDs, HFF packets, AFP acts, and Omega rules become the data and law that a deterministic Kernel, Event Horizon, Second Mind, and rule stack govern.  
- The key boundary: SGF is the substrate and protocol; SGF OS is an application on top of it, an AI operating system for governed minds built out of SGF and Omega.

### Federation, swarms, and liquid knowledge

- Independent graphs can exchange grounded Synapses, Knowledge Packs, capabilities, and receipts over HFF+AFP without sharing a database, API, or vendor, while each receiver remains sovereign over what it admits.  
- Vehicles, drones, warehouse robots, and software agents can coordinate using grounded claims plus explicit act types instead of fragile prose or one‑off APIs: “Semantic Web for machines that can say NO.”

### Auditable decision and compliance engines

- Legal, medical, and financial systems can represent testimony, rules, obligations, exceptions, and policies as Synapses with Frames and Omega constraints, so decisions and failures reconstruct as proof traces and warrant graphs rather than opaque model behavior.  
- Evidence Gates and Trust Lenses give a disciplined path from raw text or data to admitted claims, separated by source class, jurisdiction, authority tier, and epistemic status.

### Portable knowledge and governed ecosystems

- Knowledge Packs bundle SGF objects, lexicon material, and metadata (source class, domain, jurisdiction, authority tier) as signed, versioned artifacts that other systems can install, index, and reason over without treating them as unconditional truth.  
- Sidecars let legacy systems “speak SGF” at their boundaries, exposing state, answering federated queries, and eventually admitting commands over HFF+AFP without flag‑day rewrites.
