Downloading Wiktionary JSONL Data

The English Wiktionary data is hosted at kaikki.org and produced by the Wiktextract project. The current dump is from 2026-06-01. It is about 22 GB uncompressed, or 2.6 GB gzipped.

Step 1: Download the data

Open a terminal and run this command:

curl -O https://kaikki.org/dictionary/raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl

Step 2: Load into SQLite

Use the script load_wiktionary_to_db.py:

python load_wiktionary_to_db.py --source raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl --target wiktionary_raw.db --lang-code en --batch 5000
